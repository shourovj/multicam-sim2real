# Round 2: three scenes × seven conditions on Wan2.2-VACE-Fun-A14B (Nov 11, 2026)

Status: all 51 clips done. Five failed when another user's job took the same GPU during loading and were re-run.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Box control works on the two drone scenes.** All 6 box clips keep the right number of drones and follow the simulator's path. Two flaws: in 1 of 3 crossing clips the two drones swap colours as they pass, and in 1 of 3 take-off clips the drone flies in front of the tree instead of behind it.
- **The extra-drone problem from the earlier box tests did not come back.** Those tests (scripted `drones_air`, tight boxes, seed 0) gave a third drone in 3 of 3 clips. This run (physics motion, boxes padded by 6 px, seeds 0–2) gave 0 of 3. Two things changed at once, so the cause is not isolated.
- **Without control for the whole clip, the event does not happen.** Text, text + frame 0, and boxes for the first second only: in 15 of the 16 drone clips the event (the two drones passing, or the drone going behind the tree) does not happen. The exception is one P1 clip where the drones do pass.
- **Depth follows the path every time but does not hold colours.** In 4 of the 6 two-drone depth clips the orange/white pair is not kept through the crossing.
- **The convoy scene fails in every condition**, for two different reasons (§3.3). It needs rework before its clips say anything about the model.

## 2. Setup

- Model: Wan2.2-VACE-Fun-A14B (VideoX-Fun), 30 steps, 832×480, 81 frames at 16 fps, guidance 5.0.
- Scenes: `drones_air_phys`, `drone_takeoff_phys`, `convoy_overhead_phys`. Motion comes from `sim/physics`; the camera is static.
- Seeds 0, 1, 2; the text-only conditions (A, A+) have seed 0 only.
- Cost: about 13.5 min per clip on a free A100 and about 28 min on a shared one; 40.8 GiB peak allocated.

Why this scene fails:

- **Edited-frame conditions (B, B-box, P1).** The model leaves frame 0 within a few frames: it relights the scene, and in 7 of the 9 clips it also moves the camera or replaces the scene. The edited frame is dark and the vehicles are small, which may be why.
- **Depth conditions (C, C0).** From straight above, the depth map is nearly flat except for the bridge and the raised canopy strip. The model reads the strip as a solid object and draws a bridge, a wall or a huge vehicle there. The small vehicles do follow the simulator's positions.

## 4. Caveats

- Three seeds per condition, one for text-only: these are counts, not rates.
- Short events (a drone that appears for a few frames) can be missed on a 6-frame sheet. The box clips of `drones_air_phys` were also checked every 5 frames and on full frames; the others were not.
- This round differs from the PoC in more than one way: a different model, 30 steps instead of 50, physics motion, an edited frame 0 for B, B-box and P1, and padded boxes.
- Not run: Cosmos3-Nano base, D (Cosmos Sim-Transfer), E (Wan-Move), and B / B-box on the simulator frame.
